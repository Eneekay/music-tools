Tips
  • Hum the phrase however it naturally comes out — don't force it into a key you think it "should" be in.
  • Only the lowest and highest notes matter here, so it's fine to hum loosely and let the extremes register.
  • Notes need to hold steady for a moment to register, so slides between notes won't skew the result.
How This Works

This assumes your phrase's mid-range tends to sit around the 5th of the key — a common (if imperfect) rule of thumb, especially for pop and folk melodies. It's a starting point, not gospel: try the runner-up keys below too, especially if the top pick doesn't feel right.
