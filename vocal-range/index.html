Tips
  • Sing on any comfortable syllable — a hum, "ah," or "oo" all work.
  • Only comfortable, sustained notes count — don't force a strained top or bottom note.
  • Notes need to hold steady for a moment to register, so brief slides and scoops between notes won't skew your range.
  • Warm up first — your range settles after a minute of singing.
